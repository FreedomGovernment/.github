## AI Legal Assistant

Generate legal documents, analyze evidence, or get legal research assistance using your preferred LLM. Your API key is stored locally and never sent to our servers.

### Supported Models

- **OpenAI** — GPT-4o, GPT-4o Mini
- **Anthropic** — Claude 3 Haiku, Claude 3 Sonnet
- **Custom** — Any OpenAI-compatible API endpoint

### Using the AI Assistant

1. Enter your API key (stored locally in your browser)
2. Select your preferred model
3. Enter your prompt
4. Click "Generate Response"

### Quick Prompts

- Draft a complaint
- Analyze evidence
- Create a timeline
- Draft a motion to compel
- Summarize a brief
- Generate an evidence log

## How to Use the Document Converter

### Converting Text to Markdown

1. Paste your text in the input field
2. Click "Convert to Markdown"
3. Review the converted output
4. Click "Download as .md" to save the file

### Privacy

Your API key is stored only in your browser's localStorage. No data is sent to our servers. AI requests go directly from your browser to the LLM provider.
