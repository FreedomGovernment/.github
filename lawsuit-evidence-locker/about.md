# About FreeLawGen

Open-source legal document and campaign generator platform for legal activism and class action lawsuits.

## Our Mission

FreeLawGen is part of the Freedom Government initiative to provide equal access to the legal system through open-source technology. We believe that everyone deserves access to professional legal tools, regardless of their ability to afford expensive attorneys or legal software.

## How It Works

FreeLawGen is a Jekyll-based static website hosted on GitHub Pages, which means:

- **Free Hosting** — No server costs, no monthly fees, no subscriptions
- **Private by Design** — Your data stays in your browser — no servers collect or store your information
- **Open Source** — All code is publicly available for review, modification, and contribution
- **AI-Ready** — Integrated with major LLM providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) for document generation
- **Professional Templates** — Court-ready templates for complaints, petitions, motions, and more

## Technology Stack

- **Static Site Generator:** Jekyll (GitHub Pages compatible)
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages (free)
- **AI Integration:** OpenAI API, Anthropic API, or custom endpoints
- **Data Storage:** Browser localStorage (no server-side storage)

## Privacy & Security

FreeLawGen is designed with privacy as a core principle:

- All evidence and case data is stored locally in your browser
- API keys are stored in localStorage and never transmitted to our servers
- AI requests go directly from your browser to the LLM provider
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry is collected
- All code is open-source and auditable
