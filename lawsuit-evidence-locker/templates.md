## How to Use Templates

1. Browse the templates above
2. Click "Use Template" for the one you need
3. The template will load in the Document Converter
4. Replace all bracketed placeholders with your case information
5. Review and customize as needed
6. Download the finished document

## Template Tips

- **Always Review** — Templates are starting points, always review and customize for your specific case
- **Check Local Rules** — Different courts may have specific formatting requirements
- **Use AI Wisely** — The AI assistant can help draft sections, but you're responsible for accuracy
- **Keep Records** — Save copies of all documents you create
- **Consult an Attorney** — Consider consulting with a licensed attorney for complex cases
