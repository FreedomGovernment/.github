## Memorandum of law

The motion invokes Oregon Rule of Civil Procedure 71A to correct clerical mistakes in judgments and errors arising from oversight or omission, by the court on its own motion or a party's motion after notice. It argues that the judgment's mailing-address typo is clerical.

## Conclusion

Counsel says the General Judgment of Default and Money Award, signed by Judge Stephen Morgan, contains a clerical error within part C of the Money Award; Lane County requests a corrected judgment to correct the oversight.
