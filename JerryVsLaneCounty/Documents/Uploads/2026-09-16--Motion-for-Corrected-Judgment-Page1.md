# Motion for Corrected Judgment — page 1 of 2

Circuit Court of the State of Oregon for Lane County. Lane County, a Political Subdivision of the State of Oregon, plaintiff, v. Jerry Nelson, defendant. Case No. 25CV55934.

Stephanie Davidson, Assistant County Counsel, moves the court for a corrected judgment. Background states that on October 14, 2025 plaintiff filed a complaint for foreclosure of local government lien concerning property at 8620 Howe Lane, Creswell, OR 97426; on June 25, 2026 plaintiff filed its First Amended Complaint for Foreclosure of Local Government Lien; and after a June 25, 2026 default order and an August 7, 2026 general judgment of default and money award, that judgment authorized Lane County Sheriff's Office to sell the property. The motion says the judgment included a clerical error in paragraph C of the Money Award concerning the defendant's mailing address: “2525 City View Drive” instead of “2725 City View Drive.”
