## Parties, Jurisdiction, and Venue

1. Plaintiff Jerry Nelson is an Oregon resident and is identified as the owner of record of property at 82602 Howe Lane, Creswell, Oregon, 97426, in the County's foreclosure papers.

2. Defendant Lane County, hereinafter the County, is the county government that asserted and sought to foreclose a local-government nuisance lien against that property.

3. This Court has federal-question jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1331 over claims under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 and Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act ("ADA"), 42 U.S.C. § 12132. It also has jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1343 over the civil-rights claims.
