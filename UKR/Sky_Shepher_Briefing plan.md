# I. Overview

Sky Shepherd is a tiered drone warfare doctrine built on ISR superiority, electronic dominance, and precision strike capabilities. The doctrine operates across a 20 km deep battlefield using four primary UAV classes: Archangel ISR, Mali.eye ECM, LITUUS relay, and Specula (Watchtower) command. Supporting systems include Sagitta precision loitering munitions and rapid Interceptor/QRF drones.

# II. UAV Stack

\- Archangel: 7,000m ISR drone, deep recon, target identification  
- Mali.eye: 4,000m ECM unit, GNSS jamming and electronic suppression  
- LITUUS: 2,500m relay and swarm router, GPS rebroadcast and mesh control  
- Specula 'Watchtower': 4,000m C2 UAV, live C2 of QRF/Interceptors  
- Sagitta 'Arrow': Long-range loitering munition, strikes 10-20 km into enemy lines  
- Interceptor/QRF: Short-launch FPV-type drones for rapid reaction and swarm

# III. Operational Phases

1\. ISR Phase - Archangel maps enemy positions and movements  
2. EW Suppression - Mali.eye initiates jamming, denies enemy control  
3. Relay & Routing - LITUUS establishes drone net and routes commands  
4. Command Coordination - Specula confirms targets, reassigns intercept paths  
5. Strike - Sagitta launched into enemy rear, Interceptors hit frontline and mobile targets  
6. Post-Strike - Archangel conducts BDA, Specula coordinates follow-on tasks

**Sky Shepherd – Recon-Led Attack Doctrine (V1)**

**“Strike with Sight, Blind with Signal, Swarm with Certainty.”**

**I. Overview**

Objective:

Establish full-spectrum UAV dominance across a 20 km battlespace, beginning with deep ISR penetration, followed by ECM suppression, and final exploitation by swarm strike elements.

**II. UAV ROLES & DEPLOYMENT TIERS**

**1. Archangel ISR Unit**

- Type: High-altitude fixed-wing UAV

- Operational Altitude: 7,000m

- Range Envelope:

- 10km behind enemy lines

- 10km inside friendly territory

**Mission Role:**

- Persistent deep reconnaissance

- Early warning and BDA (Battle Damage Assessment)

- Real-time terrain/target mapping

**Payload:**

- EO/IR gimbal

- SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar)

- Digital zoom optics

**Survivability Tactics:**

- High-altitude flight above most MANPADs

- EMCON (Emission Control) unless transmitting via LITUUS

**2. Mali.eye EW/ECM Unit**

- Type: Mid-altitude multirole UAV

- Operational Altitude: 4,000m

- Position: Loiters between 2km–6km forward of friendly zero line

**Mission Role:**

- EW/ECM bubble over frontlines

- GNSS spoofing/degradation

- Jamming C2 links and drone feeds

**Payload:**

- Directional jammer array

- Broadband noise disruptor

- Passive RF triangulation sensors

**Support Role:**

- Protects ISR Archangel from tracking

- Supports entry of friendly strike drones (FPVs, loitering munitions)

**3. LITUUS Relay/EW Node**

- Type: Communications & EW Router UAV

- Altitude: 1,500–3,000m (adaptive based on terrain and threat)

- Position: Straddles friendly zero line; always behind Mali.eye

**Mission Role:**

- Acts as a GNSS rebroadcaster for friendly swarms

- Mesh router for encrypted FPV/telemetry/command control

- EW fallback node in case of Mali.eye loss

**Payload:**

- High-gain antenna arrays

- Onboard encryption/decryption module

- Light electronic counter-countermeasure (ECCM) suite

**Effect:**

- Enables long-range swarming without LOS comms breakdown

- Assures reliability in GPS-denied or jammed spaces

**III. Doctrine Phases**

**Phase 0: Deployment**

- Launch LITUUS first to establish comms net.

- Deploy Mali.eye to prepare electronic battlespace.

- Launch Archangel after 3-minute EW warm-up.

**Phase I: ISR & Mapping**

- Archangel penetrates deep into enemy zone (0–10km).

- Records and relays target coordinates, troop movement, and soft targets.

- Mali.eye begins GNSS degradation on enemy zones.

**Phase II: EW Domination**

- Mali.eye initiates targeted jamming of suspected enemy drone control signals.

- LITUUS rebroadcasts encrypted GNSS overlay for FPV assault units.

- Enemy comms disrupted; enemy drone capability reduced.

**Phase III: Strike Vectorization**

- Friendly swarm/FPV units launched along optimal vectors revealed by Archangel.

- LITUUS directs swarm waves in real time.

- Mali.eye rotates ECM focus based on observed threat vectors.

**Phase IV: Battle Damage & Extraction**

- Archangel circles back, scans for impact metrics.

- Mali.eye reduces ECM to assess enemy counteraction.

- LITUUS prioritizes route clearance for swarm retreat or suicide termination.

**IV. Command Notes**

**EMCON Protocols:**

- Archangel transmits only on command or LITUUS bridge.

- Mali.eye operates semi-autonomous jamming sweeps.

**Failover Logic:**

- If Mali.eye is down, LITUUS increases EW output at cost of comms fidelity.

**Redundancy:**

- Optional 2nd LITUUS to ensure uninterrupted data relay for longer ops.

**Sky Shepherd – Interceptor & QRF Doctrine (V2)**

**“Fastest to Strike. First to Cut.”**

**I. Objective**

Deploy a rapid-intercept UAV layer capable of striking targets identified by Archangel ISR and operating under the protection and data-link of the EW drones (Mali.eye & LITUUS). Positioned to act within 20km forward of friendly zero line, these systems engage time-sensitive, mobile, or high-value enemy assets.

**II. Force Composition**

**A. Interceptor Team**

Positioning:

- Forward deployed, concealed in hardened positions near the zero line

- Capable of launching within 60 seconds upon cue

**Composition:**

- 8–12 Fast Interceptor Drones

- Armed with kinetic (fragmentation) or explosive payloads (e.g., VOG-25, thermobarics)

- Optional airburst or directional charge for drone-on-drone intercepts

**Strike Envelope:**

- 0–20 km into enemy territory

- Effective loiter time of 15–20 minutes

**Cues from:**

- Archangel (targeting priority)

- LITUUS (routing/navigation)

- Mali.eye (jammed zones/timing windows)

**B. QRF Drone Units**

- Type: Modular multirole drones (rotary or VTOL preferred)

**Payload Options:**

- Anti-armor (shaped charge warheads)

- Mine-layer pods or clearing devices

- Emergency swarm repeaters (to reinforce assault)

**Role:**

- Reaction to enemy reinforcements or drones

- Route denial and pursuit of fallback units

- Support to breached sectors

**III. Deployment Protocols**

**Phase I – Standby Alert**

- Interceptors pre-fueled and awaiting command uplink from LITUUS

- Operators monitor Archangel feed in real-time

- QRF drones remain cold to avoid early detection

**Phase II – Intercept Trigger**

- Upon Archangel designation (confirmed enemy vehicle, artillery reposition, counter-drone prep), Interceptors launch in under 60 sec

- LITUUS calculates route and assigns guidance

- Mali.eye increases EW coverage along target ingress path

**Phase III – Strike Execution**

- Interceptor drone swarm enters target zone under radio silence, except via LITUUS routing

**Priority targets:**

- Mobile artillery

- Enemy drone control centers

- Troop transports or forward observers

- Post-strike video/data fed to LITUUS → back to command node via encrypted relay

**Phase IV – QRF Activation (if needed)**

- If enemy force adapts or counter-deploys:

- QRF drones scramble to designated sectors

- Lay suppressive mines, conduct hardpoint strikes, or intercept new drone waves

- Option to "burn in" enemy jammers via guided EW targeting from Mali.eye

**IV. Command Notes**

- LITUUS is critical to maintaining command-and-control over long-range Interceptors.

- Swarm Retasking: Interceptors can be redirected mid-flight if a higher-priority target emerges.

- Strike Windows: Time attacks to follow Mali.eye’s jamming pulses for minimal enemy response.

- Failsafe: Interceptors carry dead-man failsafe to detonate on command loss or on contact.

**Sky Shepherd – Doctrine Update: Specula UAV “Watchtower”**

**“The Eye That Commands the Blade.”**

**I. Designation: Specula UAV “Watchtower”**

- Type: High-endurance coordination UAV

- Altitude: 3,000–5,000m cruising

- Orbit: Rearward arc behind zero line (safe stand-off position)

- Flight Time: 12+ hours

- Comm Integration: Primary uplink node with direct feeds from Archangel, Mali.eye, LITUUS, Interceptors, and QRF units

- Redundancy Role: Secondary router if LITUUS is disabled

**II. Mission Role**

**1. Command & Coordination**

- Aggregates all UAV telemetry (ISR, ECM, intercept strikes) into a unified battlefield picture

- AI-assisted or operator-aided coordination of QRF launch timing and reroutes

- Handles multi-wave intercept tasking in concert with strike packages and terrain overlays

**2. Target Verification & Sorting**

- Receives Archangel priority targets, confirms via overlapping ISR

- Filters out decoys, false positives, and ECM noise (cross-referencing Mali.eye jamming windows)

- Passes validated targets to Interceptors with precise GPS/GNSS overlays

**3. Situational Command Node**

- Allows human or autonomous mission commander to redirect units mid-flight

- Retasking: e.g., reroute QRF units to support breach, designate emergency fallback lane

- Optional integration with satellite or high-altitude balloon feeds if available

**4. Communications Hub**

- Operates as Tier 2 relay node beneath LITUUS, able to bridge swarm control across hills, valleys, or jamming fields

- Maintains battlefield radio discipline, controlling when each asset transmits to avoid spectrum saturation

**III. Operational Benefits**

\| Feature \| Effect \|

\| \*\*Persistent Loiter\*\* \| Maintains full battlespace visibility and drone coordination throughout multi-hour operations \|

\| \*\*Multi-Asset Sync\*\* \| Eliminates C2 delay between ISR → Target → Interceptor/QRF \|

\| \*\*Strike Validation\*\* \| Ensures resources are not wasted on false contacts or traps \|

\| \*\*Drone Swarm Router Backup\*\* \| If LITUUS goes dark, Specula assumes full routing role with 60% efficiency \|

\| \*\*Real-time Reroute\*\* \| Can redirect QRF interceptors to shifting enemy armor columns or counterattack waves \|

**IV. Updated Command Stack**

\[High Altitude ISR\] → ARCHANGEL (7km)

\[EW Coverage & Disruption\] → MALI.EYE (4km)

\[Drone Link & Relay\] → LITUUS (2.5km)

\[Battlespace Oversight\] → SPECULA "WATCHTOWER" (4km orbit)

\[Direct Action Units\] → INTERCEPTORS + QRF (0–20km forward)

**Sky Shepherd – Doctrine Update: Sagitta “Arrow”**

**“Fast. Precise. Deadly.”**

**I. Designation: Sagitta UAV "Arrow"**

- Type: Loitering munition / High-speed interceptor

- Range Envelope:

- Launch zone: From friendly zero line

- Engagement zone: 10–20 km behind enemy lines

**Operational Altitude: 200–1,000m terrain-following (stealth approach)**

- Speed: 150–300 km/h (interceptor variant)

- Endurance (Loiter Mode): 20–30 mins

**Payload:**

- Shaped charge or fragmentation warhead (anti-personnel or anti-armor)

- Optional EFP (explosively formed penetrator) variant

**Guidance:**

- GNSS with inertial fallback

- Optical/IR lock or real-time operator override via LITUUS relay

- Can receive mid-flight retarget from Specula or Archangel

**II. Tactical Role**

**1. Deep Precision Strike**

- Launched post-confirmation by Archangel and Specula

- Used to destroy:

- Mobile HQs

- Artillery resupply convoys

- C2 nodes

- Air defense emplacements

Strike Depth: Beyond normal drone interdiction range

**2. Hunter-Killer Interceptor**

- Deployed to eliminate:

- Retreating high-value vehicles

- Enemy drone carriers or jammers moving to the rear

- Hardened targets trying to exit kill zone

- Optional use in drone-on-drone mid-air collisions (if enemy UAVs detected retreating or re-arming)

**3. Denial & Panic Induction**

- Sagitta waves can be timed to:

- Strike after an initial swarm assault, catching survivors or response vehicles

- Hit command elements in rear trying to reorganize after ECM disruption

- Psychological effect: makes rear lines feel unsafe and exposed, inducing retreat or hesitation

**III. Deployment Protocol**

\| Phase \| Action \|

\| \*\*Pre-Strike\*\* \| Archangel or Specula identifies key rear targets; Mali.eye jams relevant sectors. \|

\| \*\*Launch\*\* \| Sagitta units are launched in waves of 2–6 via low-profile rail or catapult systems. \|

\| \*\*Loiter or Direct-Strike\*\* \| Sagitta hovers at low altitude or loiters near predesignated zones for final confirmation. \|

\| \*\*Strike\*\* \| Engages confirmed targets, either autonomously or via operator command. \|

**IV. Chain of Command Integration**

**Sagitta Receives:**

- Target packets from Specula “Watchtower”

- Navigation/GNSS overlay from LITUUS

- Timing coordination with Mali.eye (ECM window exploitation)

- BDA (post-strike damage report) optionally relayed to Archangel

**V. Strategic Benefits**

\| Capability \| Strategic Effect \|

\| \*\*Deep Penetration\*\* \| Allows lethal reach well beyond front-line trench battles \|

\| \*\*Reduced Operator Load\*\* \| Semi-autonomous with operator override if needed \|

\| \*\*Flexible Targeting\*\* \| Can hunt, loiter, or strike depending on mission evolution \|

\| \*\*Deadly Precision\*\* \| Designed for pinpoint hits on soft or mobile targets \|
