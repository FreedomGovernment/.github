# UAV Engine Endurance Evaluation for Mali.eye

## Objective:

Identify which four-stroke engines from the provided list can realistically support 12 to 20 hours of loiter time for UAV operations.

## Engine Evaluation Summary:

| Engine                | 12–20 hr Loiter Capability | Comments                                                          |
|-----------------------|----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Enya 4-Stroke 70      | ✅ Possible                | Very efficient at low throttle, needs ~12-14L fuel for 12–18 hrs. |
| O.S. Engines FS-70    | ✅ Possible                | Efficient; needs ~11L for 12 hrs, ~18.5L for 20 hrs.              |
| O.S. Engines FT-300   | ❌ No                      | High fuel consumption (~2.4 L/hr); limited to 3-4 hrs endurance.  |
| O.S. Engines FF-300   | ❌ No                      | Similar to FT-300; endurance limited by high burn rate.           |
| Saito FA-180          | ⚠️ Difficult               | Possible with huge tanks; realistic 8–10 hrs endurance.           |
| Saito FA-182TD        | ✅ Very Good               | Excellent choice; ~1.4 L/hr; needs ~16.8L for 12 hrs.             |
| Saito FA-300T         | ❌ No                      | Too fuel hungry for 12 hr+ missions.                              |
| Saito FA-170R (3-cyl) | ✅ Possible                | Good efficiency; needs ~18L for 12 hrs.                           |
| Saito FA-325R5        | ❌ No                      | High fuel burn (~2.6 L/hr); 3-4 hrs endurance max.                |

## Fuel Requirements for 12–20 Hr Loiter:

| Engine             | Fuel Needed for 12 hrs | Fuel Needed for 20 hrs |
|--------------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Saito FA-182TD     | ~16.8L                 | ~28L                   |
| Saito FA-170R      | ~18L                   | ~30L                   |
| O.S. Engines FS-70 | ~11L                   | ~18.5L                 |
| Enya 70            | ~10L                   | ~17L                   |

## Key Observations:

\- Best engines for 12–20 hour missions are lightweight, highly efficient 4-stroke singles or small twins.

\- Heavier twin-cylinder and radial engines burn too much fuel for extreme endurance unless a very large fuel supply is installed.

\- For maximum mission time with Mali.eye, engines like the Saito FA-182TD, FA-170R, or OS FS-70 are ideal.

\- Engines like the Enya 70, OS FS-70, and Saito FA-182TD are ideal for low-cost, high-endurance UAV missions.

\- Mission role should guide engine selection: ISR platforms favor low fuel burn, ECM or strike platforms favor high thrust.
